USB Ieum Privacy Policy (English)

Effective date: November 20, 2026

CNP ("we") does not collect personal data ourselves through the Android app "USB Ieum" (com.cnp.usbieum) or the Windows program "USB Ieum". There is no account and no server operated by us.

  1. Advertising (Google AdMob). The Android app shows a banner ad at the bottom of its screen using Google AdMob (Google LLC). AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and diagnostic/crash information, and send them to Google for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where consent is required (e.g. EEA, UK), the app asks for consent on first launch and you can change it anytime with "Privacy options" in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  2. Internet traffic. The app relays the internet traffic of the USB-connected PC through the phone's network. The traffic is forwarded to its destination only; it is never stored or sent to us or any third party.
  3. On-device diagnostic logs. For troubleshooting, the app records on the device only: session start/end times, duration, amount of data and speed, network type, battery/power-saving state, device model, Android version, app version and error messages. Website addresses and traffic content are not logged. Logs rotate automatically and are deleted when the app is uninstalled. Tapping "Export logs" saves them to the device's Downloads folder; we receive logs only if you send them to us yourself.
  4. Permissions. Internet / network / Wi-Fi state: relay traffic and show the network type. Foreground service (connected device) and notifications: keep the connection while the screen is off and show its status. Wake lock: only while a PC is connected. Storage (Android 9 and lower only): save exported logs.
  5. Sharing. Advertising data described in 1 is processed by Google LLC (USA). No other sharing.
  6. Children. The app is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.
